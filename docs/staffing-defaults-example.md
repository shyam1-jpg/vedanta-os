# The Vedanta: example staffing defaults (research-backed)

*Prepared 28 Sep 2026. These are example defaults to edit, not policy. Site: 41 guest rooms + 1 staff room, about 130 max guests, buffet-only vegetarian restaurant (3 meals a day, food included), 150-seat dining room.*

**How to read:** each figure is the number of people on that shift, on a day with that many guests on site. For 7-day cover, multiply by about 1.4 to get weekly FTE (7 days / 5-day contracts), plus about 12% for holiday. Office departments (purchasing, finance/HR, sales) work fixed weekday hours and don't change with guest numbers. Anything marked *judgement call* has no good published benchmark.

**Key assumptions:** about 2 guests per room until all 41 rooms are in use (about 80 guests), then 3+ per room. Visiting retreat groups bring their own teachers. Night cover is a sleep-in duty manager, not a 24h desk. Shifts over 6h include the statutory 20-minute break.

## Restaurant / front-of-house dining

| Shift | Time | 0 | 1-15 | 16-35 | 36-60 | 61-100 | 101-130 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early FOH (breakfast + lunch buffet, tea/coffee station) | 07:00-15:00 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Late FOH (afternoon tea, dinner buffet, clear-down) | 14:00-22:00 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 5 |
| Plate-wash / FOH porter (crockery & cutlery) | 11:30-20:30 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Dining supervisor (dedicated) | 11:00-20:00 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

**Basis / rule:** Buffet attendant/clearer at ~1 per 40 guests for the lighter, staggered breakfast/lunch (early shift) and ~1 per 30 guests at dinner (late shift), rounded up, minimum 1 when guests are in. Industry buffet ratios are 1 server per 20-30 guests (1:40 lean) and 1 busser per ~25 guests with china; self-serve tea/coffee is replenished by the same staff. Dedicated plate-wash at ~1 per 80 covers per service once above ~35 guests (below that the kitchen KA/KP washes up). At 61-100 one late staffer is the designated lead; at 101-130 add a dedicated supervisor (a 'captain' for 100+ buffets).

**Notes:** The 1:40 early-shift ratio and the 20-30 minute overlap timing are judgement calls, not published figures.

## Housekeeping

| Shift | Time | 0 | 1-15 | 16-35 | 36-60 | 61-100 | 101-130 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Room attendants, changeover day (mostly departures) | 08:00-16:00 | 0 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 8 |
| Room attendants, mid-retreat day (stayovers) | 08:00-16:00 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Housekeeping supervisor / inspector | 08:00-16:30 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

**Basis / rule:** 8h shift minus 30 min break = 7.5h paid, about 6h (360 min) productive. Timings: departure 35-45 min for a standard room (we use 45-55 min because retreat rooms average ~3 guests each at full occupancy), stayover 20-25 min, plus 2-5h of public-area cleaning a day (meditation hall, corridors, toilets), with a +10% buffer. Occupied rooms assumed to be about guests/2, capped at 41 (1-15 guests: up to 8 rooms; 16-35: 18; 36-60: 30; 61+: all 41). Benchmark: 15-17 room credits per attendant per shift (departure = 1, stayover = 0.5); 3-star 16-18 rooms/shift, 4-star 13-15. Supervisor: 1 per 8-12 attendants, or 1 inspector per 4-6 attendants. Below ~3 attendants a working lead attendant inspects instead.

**Notes:** At 0 guests, deep-clean and project work is optional (judgement call). Many retreat centres only do a light stayover service or none mid-retreat; if so, the stayover line drops to about 1-2 people for public areas only.

## Front of house / reception

| Shift | Time | 0 | 1-15 | 16-35 | 36-60 | 61-100 | 101-130 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reception early | 07:30-15:30 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Reception late | 14:30-22:30 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Changeover-day arrivals overlap (group check-in window) | 12:00-18:00 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |

**Basis / rule:** The front office example assumes about 5 minutes per check-in/out, with staff sized to the peak window rather than to room count. A group of N arriving within about 3 hours needs ceil(N x 5 / 180) people at the desk: 15 guests = 1, 35 = 1, 60 = 2, 100 = 3, 130 = 4 (early/late staff plus the overlap). Pre-registering from a rooming list reduces this. At 0 guests: 1 weekday office-hours person for phones and bookings. Overnight cover is the sleep-in duty manager (see Management), not a night receptionist.

**Notes:** Keeping the desk open until 22:30 and not running a 24h desk is a judgement call for a retreat setting.

## Maintenance

| Shift | Time | 0 | 1-15 | 16-35 | 36-60 | 61-100 | 101-130 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maintenance technician (weekday day) | 08:00-16:30 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Changeover / weekend handyperson | 10:00-16:00 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |

**Basis / rule:** Benchmarks are 1.4-1.8 maintenance FTE per 100 rooms for full-service hotels and 0.5-1.3 for select/limited-service, i.e. about 0.6-0.8 FTE for 42 rooms. Rounded up to 1 full-time technician because of the commercial kitchen, 150-seat dining room and halls. Workload depends on building age and systems more than on guests, so this is largely fixed. Out-of-hours: on-call rota. At 61+ guests, add part-time cover on changeover/weekend days (judgement call). Statutory servicing (gas, fire alarm, legionella, PAT) is usually contracted out.

## Estate and grounds

| Shift | Time | 0 | 1-15 | 16-35 | 36-60 | 61-100 | 101-130 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gardener / groundsperson | 08:00-16:00 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Seasonal grounds assistant (Apr-Oct) | 08:00-16:00 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |

**Basis / rule:** Acreage-driven, not guest-driven: 1 groundsperson per 15 acres (high standard), 18 (standard) or 20 (acceptable) for basic grounds care. Assumes roughly 15-20 acres, so 1 FTE. Add a seasonal assistant in the growing season, and at high occupancy for paths, car park and event set-up (judgement call). Adjust for actual acreage and formal gardens.

## Programmes and events (retreat hosts / coordinators)

| Shift | Time | 0 | 1-15 | 16-35 | 36-60 | 61-100 | 101-130 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Programme coordinator (office: bookings, group liaison) | 09:00-17:00 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Retreat host, day (room set-up, AV, session changeovers) | 08:30-17:00 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Retreat host, evening sessions | 16:00-21:30 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 |

**Basis / rule:** Visiting groups usually bring their own teachers, so the centre supplies hosts rather than instructors. Judgement rule: 1 host per group on site during active sessions, plus 1 per extra ~50 guests or extra concurrent group. For in-house teaching, use the retreat instructor norms: 1:10-15 for a standard group retreat, 1:4-8 for premium. For comparison, corporate retreats run 3-5 support staff for 20-30 guests (all roles).

**Notes:** No published host-to-guest benchmark was found for UK retreat centres, so the host counts are a judgement call.

## Management / duty manager

| Shift | Time | 0 | 1-15 | 16-35 | 36-60 | 61-100 | 101-130 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| General manager (Mon-Fri) | 09:00-17:30 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Duty manager, early | 07:00-15:00 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Duty manager, late | 14:30-22:30 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Sleep-in duty manager / fire responder (on call, staff room) | 22:30-07:30 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

**Basis / rule:** Rule: one named person in charge whenever guests are on site. Below 36 guests the GM or a head of department covers the early slot. Overnight, UK fire safety guidance for sleeping accommodation requires enough trained staff for night-time evacuation, so there is always 1 sleep-in responder when guests are present (using the 42nd room). Above ~100 guests, consider 2 night responders per the fire risk assessment. Shifts follow UK rest-break rules (20 min break for more than 6h worked).

**Notes:** The duty-manager counts are a judgement call; the night-cover requirement comes from the fire risk assessment.

## Kitchen (SUGGESTION: extrapolated from owner's 30-35 guest pilot)

| Shift | Time | 0 | 1-15 | 16-35 | 36-60 | 61-100 | 101-130 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Morning chef | 07:00-16:00 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Late chef | 12:00-21:00 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| KA/KP early | 07:00-15:00 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| KA/KP late | 15:00-22:00 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| Relief / float | 10:00-19:00 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Head chef (Mon-Fri, menus/ordering/HACCP) | 08:00-17:00 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |

**Basis / rule:** SUGGESTION ONLY. The owner's pilot (30-35 guests) is 1 chef and 1 KA/KP per shift plus a float, which scales to about ceil(guests/35) per shift. That lands between published banquet norms (1 chef per 25 guests, 1 KA per 50, 1 KP per 100) and buffet norms (1 cook per 50-100 guests; 1 dishwasher per 80). At 1-15 guests, coverage (two shifts across 07:00-22:00) matters more than volume. The late KA/KP is held at 3 for 101-130 because the FOH plate-wash role takes the crockery. A head chef is added at 61+.
